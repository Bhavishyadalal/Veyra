# Art direction and runtime delivery

The five-column `previews/art-direction.png` was generated with OpenAI image
generation for this collection. Its source is retained at
`/workspace/generated_images/exec-ae5164b6-2462-4390-914c-6f3bd8f64311.png` in the
working environment. It is concept art, never a runtime icon atlas.

The functional pack glyphs are original SVG masters under `vectors/`, rendered
with CairoSVG at 128/256 px; fonts are real licensed font files, not AI lettering.
Specimen/all-assets sheets show these actual exports. No third-party logo or
character replaces app branding. No mascot added. Each style has a distinct frame,
line treatment, heading font and supported motion/progress profile.
